Host an arena in about a minute

You set the shape of the event. Gambit House handles pairing, fair-play checks, standings and the trophy at the end.

  1. Pick a format and clock

    Arena, Swiss or knockout, from bullet to classical. Thematic arenas start every game from a position you choose.

  2. Decide who can play

    Open to everyone, limited by rating, or private to your club with a join code.

  3. Share the link

    Spectators get live boards and standings without an account. Results post to your club page when the clock stops.
