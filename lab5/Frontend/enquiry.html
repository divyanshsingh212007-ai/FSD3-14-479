Before you write

Four questions that make up most of what lands in the inbox.

I lost access to my account. Can you reset it?

Yes, if the account has a confirmed email address. Use the recovery link on the sign-in page first — it resolves most cases in a minute. If the email is also gone, send an enquiry from the address you originally signed up with and we'll verify from there.

Can you help me run a tournament for my school or company?

We do this most weeks. Pick "Running an event" above and tell us the date and expected size. Anything under 500 players you can set up yourself from the hosting page in about a minute; above that we'll help with seeding, prize handling and a landing page.

My account was flagged for engine use and I didn't cheat.

Appeals go to a person who reviews the full case rather than a score. Send the link to the games in question and anything that explains the play — a coached line, a study you'd just worked through, a position you knew. We reverse flags we get wrong, and we say so publicly when a batch was bad.

Do you sponsor streamers, clubs or over-the-board events?

We don't pay for sponsorship, because the money comes from players. We do support events with free relay from physical boards, custom arena pages and commentary tooling. Pick "Partnership" and describe the event.
