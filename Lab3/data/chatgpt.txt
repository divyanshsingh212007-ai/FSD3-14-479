ChatGPT is an artificial intelligence assistant developed by OpenAI. It can understand and generate natural language, helping users with tasks such as answering questions, writing and editing content, brainstorming ideas, learning concepts, coding, summarizing information, and analyzing data. ChatGPT is designed to communicate conversationally, making it useful for students, professionals, developers, and everyday users. It can adapt its responses to different needs and explain complex topics in a simple and understandable way.
